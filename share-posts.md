# Shareable Microsoft Learn playlists

These are six short, audience-specific posts. Share them where they are
relevant, and invite people to study or discuss a topic rather than asking
them to open links for artificial engagement. Every link includes the
`studentamb_546883` referral code supplied for this library.

## 1. Beginner AI and agent concepts

Starting with AI agents? This is a practical beginner sequence:

1. Learn the foundations of generative AI and agents:
   https://learn.microsoft.com/training/modules/fundamentals-generative-ai/?wt.mc_id=studentamb_546883
2. Review core AI concepts:
   https://learn.microsoft.com/training/modules/get-started-ai-fundamentals/?wt.mc_id=studentamb_546883
3. Explore apps and agents on Azure:
   https://learn.microsoft.com/training/paths/get-started-ai-apps-agents/?wt.mc_id=studentamb_546883

Study question: What makes an agent different from a conventional application
that calls an AI model once?

## Sharing guidance

Use the post that matches the audience and add your own takeaway or question.
Avoid posting the same text repeatedly or asking people to open links only to
increase a metric. Meaningful discussion and completed learning are stronger
evidence of community impact.
